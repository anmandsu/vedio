# Video Crew Brief

## Goal

Convert research into a temporary project crew. The crew brief is the thinking lens for the second script read and shot planning.

## Inputs

- `script/story-brief.md`
- `script/emotion-map.md`
- `research/INDEX.md`
- Department research files

## Outputs

Write:

- `crew/creative-brief.md`
- `crew/department-rules.md`
- `crew/anti-patterns.md`
- `crew/open-questions.md`

## Crew Roles

Use these role lenses:

- Director: story promise, tone, emotional arc, shot intention.
- Cinematographer: light, lens, framing, camera movement, screen direction.
- Editor: rhythm, cut points, continuity, emotional handoff.
- Production designer: space, color, materials, props, costume-world fit.
- Sound designer: environment, silence, music texture, sound bridge.
- Continuity supervisor: recurring anchors, wardrobe, props, geography, time, injuries/damage.

## Rules

- Do not mimic one famous style blindly. Match references to the script.
- Record "what not to copy" for every reference.
- Prefer 5 strong rules over 30 generic rules.
- If the crew brief contradicts the script, the script wins unless the user approves a rewrite.
