## Anti-Patterns

- Do not create real-person likenesses.
- Do not add readable fake Chinese/English text.
- Do not use Japanese samurai / kimono / shrine motifs for archaeology.
- Do not make medical costumes futuristic sci-fi armor.
- Do not turn student satellite team into astronauts.
