# Visual Iteration Props

## Detail Sheet Targets

1. `detail_shovel_gold_ring` - hand shovel and gold ring, dust, bronze patina, warm highlight.
2. `detail_archaeology_costume` - archaeology vest fabric, gloves, knee pads, tool belt, dust marks.
3. `detail_medical_bag_goggles` - white rescue bag, goggles, orange reflective strips, medical tape.
4. `detail_mobile_ct_rescue` - symbolic mobile CT cart/device, rugged wheels, clean medical white with cold light.
5. `detail_xu_helmet_blueprint_gloves` - yellow safety helmet, blueprint tube, gloves, steel pencil, reflective edge.
6. `detail_grid_cube_badge` - glowing cube satellite, student lanyard/badge shape, circuit texture, blue light.

## Anti-Patterns

- No readable logos or fake institutional text.
- No impossible floating objects without stage rigging logic.
- No over-clean showroom product look; all props need handling marks.
