# Visual Iteration Scenes

## Scene List

1. `scene_prologue_triptych` - 序：left archaeology, center medical rescue, right engineering/GRID, three groups frozen in separate light zones.
2. `scene_archaeology_roots` - 第一场：warm earth-gold archaeology stage, low sweeping movement, hand shovel center prop.
3. `scene_medical_rescue_plateau` - 第二场：cold white plateau rescue stage, wind, six figures, bags, mobile CT suggestion.
4. `scene_engineer_rebar_ladder` - 第三场 A：Xu Yingxue among steel/rebar ladder light rods, blueprint opening.
5. `scene_grid_cube_satellite` - 第三场 B：student circle around glowing cube satellite, second science building window implied by lit rectangle.
6. `scene_final_convergence` - 终章：all groups converge center stage, shared light, three symbolic props meet.
7. `scene_epilogue_followspot` - 尾声：one young student in a single followspot, black stage depth and fading light path.

## Stage Design Anchors

- Use black-box theater depth, visible floor tape or subtle stage seams, haze for light beams.
- Lighting can be report-hall feasible: area washes, practical glowing props, one followspot.
- Avoid hyper-real location replacement; these are designed stage worlds.
