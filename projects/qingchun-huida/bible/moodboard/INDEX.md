# Moodboard Index

## Unified Stage Design Rules

- Overall: contemporary Chinese stage concept design for a recital drama, not historical costume drama and not generic poster art.
- Color lanes:
  - Archaeology: earth brown, bronze green patina, muted gold, dust gray.
  - Medical rescue: snow white, cold blue-white, emergency orange, reflective gray.
  - Engineering / GRID: electric blue, blueprint cyan, steel gray, black stage void.
- Shape language:
  - Archaeology: low crouched silhouettes, circular gold ring, hand shovel, dust and ritual arcs.
  - Medical rescue: diagonal forward motion, rectangular bags, goggles, orange rescue strips, breath in cold air.
  - Engineering / GRID: vertical ladders, blueprint tubes, luminous cube, grid lines, modular lab geometry.
- Stage rule: every image should read as a designed stage production with visible light beams, floor marks, practical props and black-box negative space.
