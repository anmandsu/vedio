## Shared Style Lock

Use case: stylized-concept / photorealistic-natural hybrid

Asset type: brand concept visual for a Guochao Chinese opera styling experience shop in Chengdu Eastern Suburb Memory.

Global style:

- Photorealistic fashion editorial, not cartoon, not illustration.
- Chinese opera headwear is the primary subject: phoenix crown or marshal helmet must be instantly recognizable.
