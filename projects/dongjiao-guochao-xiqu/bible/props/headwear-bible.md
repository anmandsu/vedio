# 凤冠与帅盔道具圣经

## 凤冠 Design Rules

- Silhouette: high fan-shaped crown, wide enough to read in thumbnail.
- Centerpiece: phoenix motif, not generic flower-only crown.
- Texture: layered metal filigree, beads, pearls, tassels, red or black jewel accents.
- Movement: tassels must move when the wearer turns; short video needs this motion.
- Weight:快闪款轻量，写真款可更复杂但需控制颈部负担。

## 帅盔 Design Rules

- Silhouette: helmet body plus crest, red tassel or plume, clear forehead plate.
- Identity: reads as marshal/general, not motorcycle helmet or fantasy armor.
- Texture: matte black or silver base, aged gold trim, red cord, bead or ball decorations.
- Movement: red tassel/plume should show direction in turning shots.
- Comfort: internal padding and adjustable strap required for customer use.

## Procurement / Build Notes

- 快闪款：轻量树脂、泡棉、亚克力、仿金属喷漆。
- 写真款：更高密度珠穗、金属片、仿点翠、可拆卸披帛。
- 双人款：两套头饰轮廓要互补，一个高宽，一个锐利上扬。

## Avoid

- Plastic yellow gold.
- Loose beads that fall during wear.
- Heavy crown without neck support.
- Mislabeling every headpiece as "京剧" when the design is mixed国潮改良。
