# 体验馆空间视觉圣经

## Overall Look

东郊记忆国潮戏曲体验馆应有三层视觉：前景是戏曲凤冠/帅盔，中景是妆造和服装，背景是工业遗址和国潮摄影棚。空间不是古风装修，而是当代工业剧场。

## Scene 1: Red-Gold Opera Stage

- Use: 凤冠线、生日款、标准套餐。
- Palette: deep red, antique gold, warm black.
- Materials: red velvet curtain, aged wood platform, brushed gold trim, hanging bead strings.
- Lighting: warm key light 4000K, soft fill, gold rim light.
- Props: folding fan, water sleeves, small drum, opera stool.
- Avoid: overly clean wedding studio look.

## Scene 2: Black-Gold Martial Field

- Use: 帅盔线、女将军、黑金女王。
- Palette: carbon black, antique gold, dark red.
- Materials: black steel, textured matte wall, faux armor plates, old brick floor.
- Lighting: directional side light, red rim light, low haze.
- Props: command flag, spear silhouette, cape rack, armor fragments.
- Avoid: fantasy game armor or Japanese samurai cues.

## Scene 3: Industrial Neon Alley

- Use: 双人款、短视频、东郊记忆外景衔接。
- Palette: brick red, metal gray, warm gold, controlled cyan neon.
- Materials: exposed brick, steel pipe, riveted panels, old factory signage texture.
- Lighting: mixed warm key light and cool neon accent, real contact shadows.
- Props: industrial door, metal staircase, old speaker, cable lines.
- Avoid: generic cyberpunk city; keep Chinese opera headwear dominant.

## Scene 4: Sichuan Floral Window

- Use: 川剧花旦凤冠、亲子研学、轻量快闪。
- Palette: warm white, cinnabar red, blue-green, bamboo green.
- Materials: carved window, translucent paper, bamboo shadows, embroidered fabric.
- Lighting: high-key soft daylight 4500K, visible shadow detail.
- Props: face-paint color swatches, small mirror, flower hairpins.
- Avoid: museum display or classroom feel.

## Spatial Notes

- 80-150 sqm can work if the photography zones share lighting rails.
- Front display should be visible from storefront to trigger walk-ins.
- Makeup mirrors need clean light; photography zones can be more dramatic.
- Storefront should show one凤冠 and one帅盔 silhouette at all times.
