# Scene: EP01 Wedding Entrance

## Narrative Function

Public humiliation becomes public reversal. The space must make 沈婉秋 feel watched before she takes control.

## Spatial Map

- Vertical red carpet path from wedding car to entrance.
- Guests line both sides, many partially blurred or cropped.
- Wedding car door/edge can appear in foreground for opening shots.
- Bodyguards later split the background crowd for 陆之琛's entrance.

## Light And Time

- Daytime.
- Bright premium daylight, high readability, clean skin and fabric detail.
- Apparent contrast around 2.5:1-3:1; avoid heavy noir shadows.
- Veil highlights controlled so white fabric does not blow out.

## Color And Materials

- Red carpet as pressure signal.
- White/ivory bridal fabric.
- Black suits/bodyguards.
- Metallic gold/silver details used sparingly.
- Modern luxury surfaces, not palace fantasy.

## Recurring Props

- Bridal veil.
- Red carpet.
- Phone recording.
- High heel.
- Black leather shoes.

## Camera-Friendly Anchors

- Wedding car door edge.
- Veil over eyes.
- Hand/nail in palm.
- Foot trap and ankle catch.
- Heel flying.
- Black leather shoes at red carpet end.

## Sound Bed

Crowd murmur, phone recording texture, heel impact, brief silence drop before 陆之琛 entrance.
