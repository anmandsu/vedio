# Props: EP01 Anchor Props

## Bridal Veil

- Function: hides and reveals 沈婉秋's identity.
- Visual lock: translucent white/ivory tulle, visible eyes through veil, pearl/silver detail.
- Anti-pattern: opaque veil, fantasy headdress, messy cloth covering eyes.

## Red Carpet

- Function: social arena and public humiliation stage.
- Visual lock: strong vertical leading line in 9:16 frame.
- Anti-pattern: church aisle, empty carpet without social pressure.

## Phone Recording

- Function: makes humiliation public and viral.
- Visual lock: phone held by guest, screen glow or camera UI suggested without readable text.
- Anti-pattern: gibberish text, oversized UI, impossible hand anatomy.

## High Heel

- Function: villain trap and physical reversal payoff.
- Visual lock: elegant heel, contact with gown/carpet, flying heel after fall.
- Anti-pattern: unclear shoe shape, impossible foot angle.

## Black Leather Shoes

- Function: first signal of 陆之琛's arrival.
- Visual lock: polished black leather shoes stopping at red-carpet end.
- Anti-pattern: casual sneakers, ornate footwear, muddy ground.
