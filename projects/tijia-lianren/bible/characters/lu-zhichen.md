# Character: 陆之琛

## Script Anchors

- EP01: appears after bodyguards split the crowd.
- EP01: black leather shoes stop at the end of the red carpet before full reveal.
- EP01: black suit, cold expression, asks whether the bride sent by 沈家 is her.

## Identity Lock

- Age: late 20s to early 30s.
- Body / face: tall, lean, severe presence; sharp jawline; refined Korean-drama CEO grooming but clearly Chinese identity; controlled gaze.
- Hair: dark, short, clean Korean premium CEO styling, neat volume, no messy idol softness.
- Costume base: black or charcoal tailored suit, crisp white shirt, minimal accessories; slim Korean luxury tailoring, exact shoulder fit, clean lapel line.
- Signature prop: polished black leather shoes, cuff/watch detail, bodyguard entourage.
- Behavior: stillness, measured gaze, low-volume authority.
- Reference direction: Liu Xiaoxu-style Chinese short-drama leading man energy: mature clean-cut face, calm "CEO husband" restraint, firm jaw, deep-set gaze, understated wealth, no greasy霸总 exaggeration. Use as temperament and feature direction only; keep the character original.

## Continuity Anchors

- Severe black-suit silhouette.
- Authority through restraint, not loud gestures.
- Enters through partial reveals: shoe, cuff, shoulder line, then face.

## Variation Rules

- Public business/wedding: black or charcoal suit.
- Private scenes: still tailored, can loosen tie/collar only when intimacy or injury changes the power balance.
- Korean styling layer: low-saturation black/charcoal/navy palette, sharp tailoring, minimal watch/cuff detail, controlled skin finish, no flamboyant jewelry.

## Anti-Patterns

- Do not make him flashy, smiling, idol-like, or jewelry-heavy.
- Do not use patterned suits or flamboyant colors.
- Do not overuse low angle so much that the face distorts.
- Do not generate a 1:1 likeness of Liu Xiaoxu; use the short-drama male-lead temperament, not identity copy.
