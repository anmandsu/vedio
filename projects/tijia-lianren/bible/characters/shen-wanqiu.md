# Character: 沈婉秋

## Script Anchors

- EP01: sits in the wedding car under a bridal veil, eyes cold.
- EP01: absorbs 沈婉柔's insult without breaking, then counters with a quiet threat.
- EP01: catches the ankle trap, reverses the public humiliation, lifts the veil, and declares she is not a substitute to be controlled.

## Identity Lock

- Age: mid-20s.
- Body / face: slender, long-necked, sharp-boned, cold elegant face; refined Korean-drama-inspired grooming but clearly Chinese identity; strong eyes; controlled expression.
- Hair: dark hair styled with Korean premium drama cleanliness, smooth restrained updo or sleek low bun for wedding, no messy loose romance unless scene calls for emotional breakdown.
- Costume base: white or ivory bridal look, translucent veil, pearl/silver detail, cool-toned fabric; silhouette should feel high-end Korean bridal editorial, not fantasy costume.
- Signature prop: bridal veil in EP01; later can shift to phone/evidence file depending on revenge plot.
- Behavior: stillness before attack, precise eye contact, quiet voice with blade edge.

## Continuity Anchors

- Cold controlled eyes.
- Elegant but dangerous bridal silhouette.
- Minimal emotional leakage until a revenge-memory trigger.
- Uses micro-reactions: eye lift, hand tension, still posture.

## Variation Rules

- Wedding: white/ivory bridal styling with veil.
- Investigation/revenge: tailored modern clothing, clean lines, cool palette.
- Emotional collapse episodes can loosen hair/costume, but face identity and cold eye shape must persist.
- Korean styling layer: low-saturation palette, refined glass-skin makeup with visible skin texture, slim silhouette, minimal jewelry, clean hairline and precise costume fit.

## Anti-Patterns

- Do not make her soft, sweet, naive, or romance-first in EP01.
- Do not overdo tears before the plot earns them.
- Do not create exaggerated fantasy phoenix-crown styling unless chosen later.
- Do not make bridal veil hide the eyes.
