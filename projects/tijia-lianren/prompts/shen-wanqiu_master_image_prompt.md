## Prompt

Create a 9:16 vertical premium Chinese vertical short drama still, photorealistic, designed as a character bible master image for 沈婉秋.

Subject: a mid-20s Chinese woman sitting inside the back seat of a luxury wedding car, shown in an intimate closeup from upper chest to head. She has refined sharp-boned Chinese features, cold elegant almond-shaped eyes, natural double eyelids, a high clean nose bridge, a small sharp jawline, realistic cool ivory skin with visible subtle pores and fine skin texture, natural dewy but restrained bridal makeup, pale pink lips closed, no smile. Her gaze is direct but controlled, calm and dangerous, as if she has already calculated the counterattack.

Costume and hair: ivory-white luxury bridal gown visible at the neckline and shoulder, translucent white tulle bridal veil covering part of her face but the eyes remain the sharpest element. Fine pearl and cold silver edge details on the veil, realistic tulle mesh texture, soft folds near cheek and shoulder. Dark hair in a clean restrained wedding updo, no messy romantic softness.

Camera: 9:16 vertical smartphone-drama closeup, 50mm portrait perspective, eye-level inside the car, shallow depth of field, sharp focus on both eyes through the veil. The car window frame and dark leather seat create a vertical frame around her face. Slight reflection on the side window, but reflection does not cover the eyes.

Lighting: premium daylight filtered through car window, cool-neutral 5600K, soft high-key light from camera-left/front-left, gentle fill from the car interior, apparent contrast ratio 2.5:1 maximum, full detail visible in veil and skin shadows, controlled highlights on white fabric, crisp catchlights in eyes.

Background: blurred luxury car interior, dark leather seat, subtle chrome trim, no visible brand logo, no readable text.

Style: premium Chinese vertical revenge short drama, clean high-end phone-drama image, polished but emotionally sharp, realistic fabric wrinkles, realistic veil translucency, natural skin texture, no airbrushing.

no smile, no soft innocent bride mood, no fantasy phoenix crown, no palace costume, no Japanese or Korean wedding styling, no opaque veil hiding eyes, no random Chinese text, no brand logos, no plastic skin, no over-smoothed face, no digital over-sharpening, no doll face, no anime, no theatrical crying, no exaggerated expression, no deformed hands, no extra fingers, no watermark, no text overlay.
