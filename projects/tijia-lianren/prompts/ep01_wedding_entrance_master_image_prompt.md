## Prompt

Create a 9:16 vertical premium Chinese vertical short drama scene still, photorealistic, designed as the mother scene for EP01 wedding entrance.

Scene: exterior entrance of a modern Chinese luxury villa or private event venue during a daytime wedding. A red carpet runs vertically from the bottom foreground toward the entrance, creating a strong 9:16 leading line. A luxury wedding car door edge appears in the near foreground at one side. Guests stand on both sides of the carpet, some holding phones as if secretly recording, but their faces and phones are softly blurred. Two black-suit bodyguards stand near the far end, creating a future entrance path for 陆之琛.

Design: modern urban Chinese wealth, polished stone or pale marble entrance, glass/metal details, tasteful floral arrangements in white and pale champagne, controlled gold/silver accents. The space must look expensive and contemporary, not a Western church, not a palace, not a hotel lobby overloaded with decorations.

Camera: vertical smartphone-drama frame, 9:16, camera at standing eye level, 35mm natural perspective, red carpet centered as a vertical line, foreground car edge and veil/fabric hint optional, midground guests simplified, background entrance readable. One clear visual hierarchy: red carpet path first, social pressure second, luxury entrance third.

Lighting: bright premium daylight, 5600K-6200K, high-key clean exposure, apparent contrast ratio 2.5:1 maximum, transparent shadows with full detail, crisp highlights on car paint, glass, metal, and white floral surfaces. Red carpet saturated but not neon, white flowers and marble retain detail.

Atmosphere: public humiliation stage before a revenge reversal, expensive but tense, phone-viewer legibility, clean short-drama brightness, slight background blur.

No main characters in full focus; this is a scene mother image for later character placement and shot planning.

## Negative Prompt

no western church, no castle, no palace fantasy, no overdecorated gold mansion, no random Chinese text, no unreadable signage, no brand logos, no empty carpet, no crowded chaotic background, no dark noir lighting, no muddy shadows, no cartoon/anime, no distorted cars, no floating objects, no malformed hands on phones, no watermark, no text overlay.
