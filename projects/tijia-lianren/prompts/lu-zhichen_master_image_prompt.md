## Prompt

Create a 9:16 vertical premium Chinese vertical short drama still, photorealistic, designed as a character bible master image for 陆之琛.

Subject: a late-20s to early-30s Chinese man standing at the end of a luxury wedding red carpet, shown from mid-torso to head in a slightly low-angle vertical frame. He is tall, lean, severe, with refined sharp Chinese features, clean jawline, straight nose bridge, controlled dark eyes, short dark hair styled neatly back, no beard. His expression is cold, restrained, unreadable; authority comes from stillness, not aggression.

Costume: perfectly tailored black suit with crisp shoulders and narrow lapel, white shirt, black tie, minimal silver watch or cuff detail, polished black leather shoes implied by the lower-frame entrance language. No noisy patterns, no flashy jewelry.

Camera: 9:16 vertical smartphone-drama upper-body closeup, slight low angle from chest height, 65mm portrait compression, shallow depth of field. Bodyguards and wedding guests are blurred behind him, forming a parted crowd. Composition keeps him centered but slightly above the viewer, with a clean vertical line from suit lapel to gaze.

Lighting: bright premium daylight red-carpet event, cool-neutral 5400K, crisp edge light on black suit shoulders, soft front fill so facial detail remains visible, apparent contrast ratio 3:1 maximum. Black suit must keep fabric detail, not become a flat black block.

Background: modern luxury wedding entrance, red carpet blur, bodyguard silhouettes in black suits, no readable signage, no church interior, no palace fantasy.

Style: premium Chinese vertical short drama still, CEO authority entrance, sharp clean image, realistic fabric texture, natural skin texture, high-end but not fashion-magazine flamboyant.

no smile, no idol-boy sweetness, no flamboyant jewelry, no patterned suit, no leather jacket, no sunglasses, no fantasy mafia styling, no western church wedding, no random Chinese text, no brand logos, no plastic skin, no over-smoothed face, no anime, no exaggerated low-angle distortion, no deformed hands, no extra fingers, no watermark, no text overlay.
