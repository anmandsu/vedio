# Department Rules

## Director

- Opening rule: first 3 seconds must contain status conflict, humiliation, danger, or a power question.
- Revenge rule: every major insult needs a visible counterweight within the same scene unless it is explicitly deferred as a hook.
- Male-lead rule: 陆之琛 should be introduced by authority signals before full dialogue.

## Cinematographer

- 9:16 default frame: one dominant face plus one readable anchor.
- Use environment in vertical layers, not wide scenic coverage.
- Use inserts for hand, shoe, phone, veil, ring, file, contract, blood, or evidence.

## Editor

- Cut on power shifts, not just line endings.
- Keep most dialogue shots short; let the reaction shot sell the line.
- Action beats should be decomposed into setup, turn, impact, reaction.

## Production Designer

- Modern luxury must be legible but not noisy.
- Avoid uncontrolled Chinese text, unless the text is manually designed.
- Costume hierarchy: heroine cold-control, male lead severe-control, villains performative-wealth.

## Sound Designer

- Sound marks power: crowd, phone camera, heel impact, room tone drop, low entrance hit.
- Avoid constant music wall; use short stingers and half-beat drops.

## Visual Continuity

- Character image generation must produce bible-ready anchors, not one-off pretty portraits.
- Scene generation must begin with mother scenes, then variations.
