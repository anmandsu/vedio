# Creative Brief

## Project North Star

9:16 premium vertical revenge short drama. The goal is not restrained realism; the goal is high legibility, fast emotional payoff, and a polished enough world that the revenge fantasy feels expensive.

Every scene should answer one phone-viewer question:

- Who has power right now?
- What humiliation just happened?
- How will 沈婉秋 reverse it?
- Why should I watch the next episode?

## Department Doctrine

### Director

- When a scene begins, open on a pressure event or social threat because short-drama attention is earned immediately.
- When 沈婉秋 is attacked, show the insult, her controlled micro-reaction, and then the reversal because that is the core pleasure loop.
- When 陆之琛 appears, delay the full reveal through shoes, suit edge, bodyguard movement, or a low-angle partial because status should arrive before exposition.

### Cinematography

- When shooting dialogue, prefer closeup, extreme closeup, and upper-body closeup because vertical drama loses the power of conventional middle shots.
- When showing location, compress it into vertical layers behind the face because phone viewers need setting and emotion in one glance.
- When an action beat occurs, split it into object/hand/face/impact inserts because AI video and short-drama edits both handle small readable beats better.

### Editing

- When a line changes power, cut to reaction immediately because viewer satisfaction comes from seeing the emotional ledger update.
- When the episode reaches a hook, stop on a face-off or unanswered question because the ending must be a payment trigger.
- When scene information is complex, use insert shots before explanation because phone viewers need proof before dialogue.

### Production Design

- When designing luxury, choose polished modern Chinese wealth: villa entrance, red carpet, black suits, ivory bridal fabric, controlled gold/metal details.
- When designing villains, allow slightly louder styling because their social performance should contrast with 沈婉秋's cold control.
- When designing the male lead, keep wardrobe severe and minimal because authority should feel contained, not decorative.

### Sound

- When social humiliation happens, use crowd murmur and phone-recording texture because public witness raises stakes.
- When the heroine calculates, thin the sound bed briefly because internal control is her power.
- When the episode hooks, use a short suspended hit instead of a long emotional swell because the cut should feel addictive.

### Continuity

- When a character recurs, lock face, hair, costume logic, status prop, and emotional temperature before image generation.
- When a location recurs, lock one mother frame and only then generate time or mood variants.
- When a prop is important, treat it as a continuity anchor, not decoration.

## Non-Negotiables

- Aspect ratio: 9:16 vertical.
- Style: short-drama爽感 first, Korean premium polish second, prestige-drama restraint third.
- Korean premium direction means clean cool daylight, low-saturation luxury, precise hair/makeup, glass-skin but realistic texture, controlled expressions, and uncluttered modern wealth.
- No character or scene image generation until 1A physical and 1B atmosphere searches are logged and visual observations are written.
- First visual bible priority: 沈婉秋, 陆之琛, EP01 wedding entrance.
