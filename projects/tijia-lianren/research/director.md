# Director Research

## Chosen Director Form

Use a premium vertical revenge-drama showrunner model:

- Protect the phone viewer's first three seconds.
- Make every scene promise a reversal.
- Keep faces, hands, phones, shoes, veils, contracts, and injuries readable in 9:16.
- Let the emotional engine be simple and strong: humiliation, counterattack, charged eye contact, hook.

This is not an auteur slow-burn project. It can borrow polished lighting and costume from long drama, but the scene rhythm must remain short-drama sharp.

## Executable Rules

- When an episode opens, start with an active social pressure or visual humiliation because the viewer must understand the power imbalance before deciding to continue.
- When 沈婉秋 is insulted, hold one short beat on her face or hand before the counterattack because the pleasure comes from seeing control return to her.
- When a villain overacts, let the villain occupy the frame briefly, then cut to the heroine's colder reaction because the contrast makes her feel more powerful.
- When 陆之琛 enters, stage him through an object or partial body first, such as black shoes, cuff, silhouette, or shoulder line, because delayed reveal gives him 大佬 weight without long exposition.
- When an episode ends, stop on unresolved eye contact, a new threat, or a power question because the hook is the payment engine.

## What Not To Copy

- Do not copy prestige-drama patience: long empty corridors, slow silent walks, and scenic establishing shots should be rare.
- Do not let realism flatten the revenge pleasure. The performances can be heightened as long as the face and objective remain clear.
- Do not turn every shot into a beauty closeup. The short-drama grammar needs evidence inserts: phone screen, shoe, veil, hand, ring, contract, blood, file.
