# Production Design Research

## Design Position

Premium short-drama, not slow luxury ad. The world must read instantly on a phone:

- 沈家/陆家: money, status, polished surfaces, controlled color.
- 沈婉秋: cold bridal beauty with revenge edge.
- 沈婉柔/沈家: performative wealth, slightly louder styling, social cruelty.
- 陆之琛: black suit, precise tailoring, minimal jewelry/watch, restrained authority.

## Color System v0

- EP01 wedding: ivory, red carpet, black suits, metallic gold, cool skin highlights.
- Heroine: white bridal look with cold silver/pearl details; red is environmental pressure, not her emotional warmth.
- Villain sister: warmer, more decorated, more performative.
- Male lead: black/charcoal/white, sharp line, no noisy pattern.

## Physical Reference Needs Before Images

- 1A: modern Chinese villa wedding entrance, luxury red carpet arrangement, bridal car interior, Chinese high-end wedding gown/veil details, bodyguard/security entrance.
- 1B: bright luxury daylight wedding atmosphere, high-contrast but clean short-drama closeup lighting, red carpet social event background blur.

## Anti-Patterns

- Do not make the wedding look like a Western church unless the script later asks for it.
- Do not overuse palace/gilded fantasy luxury; this is modern urban wealth.
- Do not let Chinese text on signs become random; avoid visible text unless controlled.
