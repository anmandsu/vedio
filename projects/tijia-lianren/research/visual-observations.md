# Visual Observations

Project: 替嫁恋人：大佬她只想复仇
Target: EP01 image-2 preparation

## 1A Physical Reality Observations

### Bridal Car And Veil

Source: `research/physical_refs/pexels_bride_veil_car.json`

- Car interior closeups work best when the window edge or seat line creates a vertical frame around the bride's face.
- Veil translucency must not hide the eyes; the eyes should remain the sharpest element.
- White fabric needs micro-texture: tulle mesh, pearl/silver edge detail, soft folds near cheek and shoulder.
- Reflections on glass can add premium feeling, but the face cannot be obscured.
- For 沈婉秋, the bridal car image should not be soft-romantic; it should be cold, still, and controlled.

### Black Suit Authority

Source: `research/physical_refs/pexels_black_suit_event.json`

- Authority reads through tailoring: sharp lapel, crisp white shirt edge, cuff/hand adjustment, clean black fabric.
- Partial-body reveal is useful for 陆之琛: shoes, cuff, torso edge, then face.
- Avoid noisy patterns or decorative luxury. Power should feel contained and precise.
- A low camera can help, but the face must not become distorted.

### Wedding Red Carpet Structure

Source: `research/physical_refs/pexels_luxury_wedding_red_carpet.json`

- Red carpet aisle gives strong vertical leading lines for 9:16.
- Guest rows should be blurred or simplified; phone viewers need one subject, not a complex crowd.
- Daylight can support bright short-drama readability, but shadows must stay clean.
- Current references are not China-specific enough; use only for structure, not final design taste.

## 1B Atmosphere Observations

Sources:

- `research/atmosphere_refs/pexels_daylight_wedding_ceremony.json`
- `research/atmosphere_refs/pexels_red_carpet_daylight_event.json`
- `research/atmosphere_refs/pexels_luxury_event_closeup_daylight.json`

- EP01 wedding should feel bright, expensive, socially exposed, and slightly cold.
- Light target: high-key daylight around 5200K-6200K, but with controlled highlight rolloff on white dress and veil.
- Contrast target: moderate 2.5:1-3:1 apparent ratio, not dark prestige-drama contrast.
- Background density: enough guests/phones/bodyguards to signal public pressure, but not enough to distract from face.
- Red carpet must pop, but red should feel like social pressure rather than romantic warmth.
- Luxury texture can be suggested with crisp glass/metal highlights and clean white event surfaces, but the main frame must remain face-led.
- For 9:16, use red carpet or doorway lines as vertical perspective guides, not horizontal crowd geography.

## Prompt-Ready Constraints

- Always state `9:16 vertical frame`.
- Use `premium Chinese vertical short drama still`.
- Use closeup/upper-body closeup/extreme closeup instead of generic cinematic wide shot.
- Include one anchor object per image: veil, phone, red carpet, heel, black leather shoe, cuff.
- Negative: no random Chinese text, no church-like western wedding hall, no overdecorated palace luxury, no plastic skin, no airbrushed face.
