# Editing Research

## Rhythm Doctrine

The edit should feel like controlled acceleration: every 3-5 seconds something changes in power, information, or emotional pressure.

## Executable Rules

- When a line lands as humiliation, cut immediately to the target's micro-reaction because short-drama satisfaction depends on visible emotional accounting.
- When a counterattack starts, split action into setup, reversal, impact, reaction because it keeps AI generation easier and lets the edit sell power.
- When an object carries plot information, use a clean insert before the line that explains it because phone viewers need visual proof.
- When a scene is dialogue-heavy, alternate closeup, extreme closeup, insert, reaction rather than conventional shot/reverse-shot middle shots.
- When an episode reaches its card point, end on a still-readable face-off, not on movement blur.

## EP01 Editing Shape

- Hook: wedding replacement gossip plus the bride's cold eyes.
- First reversal: 沈婉秋 answers 沈婉柔's insult with a threat.
- Physical payoff: foot trap becomes ankle catch, push, fall, heel flying.
- Status escalation: 陆之琛 enters after the heroine has already overturned the social frame.
- Hook: `沈家送来的新娘...就是你?` answered by `陆总后悔了?`
