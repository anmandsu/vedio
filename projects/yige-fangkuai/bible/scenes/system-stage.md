# Scene: System Stage

## Narrative Function

The stage is the inside of a dictionary-revision system where characters are sorted, checked, revised, threatened, and archived.

## Spatial Map

- 16:9 proscenium composition.
- Clean open floor with visible traffic lanes.
- Low modular cube/block units form queues, gates, archive shelves, information islands, and final glyph composition.
- No suspended scenic pieces and no projected surfaces.

## Light And Time

- Bright high-key theatrical wash.
- Low contrast, transparent shadows.
- Cinnabar accents only for warning/deletion beats.

## Color And Materials

- Warm white, rice-paper white, pale gray, ink gray.
- Pale jade for order/archive, cinnabar for warning, restrained bronze/gold for cultural memory.
- Matte painted plywood, translucent acrylic edges, paper cards, shallow engraved glyph lines, rubber wheels.

## Recurring Props

- Modular cube/block units.
- Identity cards.
- Low gate/screening frame built from blocks.
- Archive slots and card shelves.

## Camera-Friendly Anchors

- Full 16:9 width visible.
- Actor hands on blocks visible during transitions.
- Final stage picture must show modules assembled into a square-character effect.
- Current client direction: final assembled glyph should read as `合`, built from Rubik-like interlocking modular units.

## Sound Bed

Keyboard taps, card flips, wheel rolls, light wooden knocks, soft procedural notification tones.
