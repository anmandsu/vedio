# Prop: Identity Cards

## Role

Identity cards are the show's evidence system. They represent字义、出处、审核资格、归档许可 and human scholarly labor.

## Design Lock

- Paper/card texture, warm white base, neat black typography or hand-written style marks.
- Cinnabar edge or stamp for warnings and unresolved分歧.
- Pale jade or bronze stamp for accepted/archived cards.
- Cards should be large enough for stage readability.

## Continuity Anchors

- 白 begins without a card.
- Other characters' confidence is tied to already having cards.
- 大天白亮's card transfer is the key emotional action.
- 白 receiving a card is the final acceptance beat.

## Anti-Patterns

- Cards as loose decorative paper rain.
- Tiny unreadable slips.
- Digital cards or projected cards.
