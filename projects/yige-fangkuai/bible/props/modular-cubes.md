# Prop: Modular Cubes

## Role

The modular cubes are the physical engine of the show: dictionary system, stage architecture, glyph fragments, archive shelves, gates, and final square-character composition.

## Design Lock

- 45-60 cm stage-scale cube or rectangular block units.
- Matte white / pale gray base with ink-gray edges.
- Future master direction should avoid heavy solid-box / crate feeling.
- Prefer lightweight high-design construction: hollow frames, layered panels, slim structural ribs, translucent pale-jade acrylic edges, precise joinery, and intentional negative space.
- Client note: increase Rubik's-cube-like拼接感. Modules should read as interlocking units that can rotate, split, and recombine, not just large doorway frames.
- Some faces carry removable or flip-over identity-card panels.
- Some edges contain shallow glyph-stroke grooves inspired by oracle / seal forms.
- Visible handholds are allowed and should look intentional.
- Low-profile wheels allowed if hidden enough to keep the design clean.

## Transformation Logic

- Push into line: sorting.
- Rotate face: system update or definition reveal.
- Split into lanes:分歧 /审核.
- Combine into gate: deletion or modification screening.
- Stack low: archive wall.
- Assemble flat/frontally: final方块字 effect.
- Current final glyph target: assemble into the Chinese character `合`.
- Rotate quarter-turn / slide-lock: Rubik-like reconfiguration beat.

## Anti-Patterns

- Rough rehearsal-box look.
- Heavy dark wood.
- Heavy solid cabinet blocks.
- Crude storage-box feeling.
- Hanging or flying cube elements.
- LED-screen cube faces.
- Projection-mapped glyphs.
