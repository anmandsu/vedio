# Unified Costume System

## Design Direction

All character looks must feel like one stage ensemble from the same world: high-end, quiet, light, poetic, and actor-friendly. No one should look like a separate game skin or armor hero.

## Shared Base

- Warm white / paper white / pale gray base.
- Soft layered contemporary stagewear: long sleeveless vest, wide trousers, light robe-coat, or wrap panel.
- Translucent pale-jade acrylic or organza edge details used sparingly.
- Thin ink-gray seam lines forming square grids or radical fragments.
- One tiny cinnabar seal/card accent per character at most.
- Movement-friendly silhouettes for pushing, rotating, crouching, and passing cards.

## Differentiation Rules

- Distinguish characters by one restrained accent: proportion, sleeve shape, collar shape, one color note, or one symbolic panel.
- Do not use heavy armor, fantasy shoulder pieces, large sunburst chest pieces, or busy design-sheet text.
- Keep the face / posture alive and human; 灵气 comes from gaze, breath, asymmetry, and a small personal gesture.
