# Creative Brief

## Project North Star

把《一个方块》做成一个明快、干净、高级的实体舞台寓言：没有悬挂道具，没有投影效果，所有“系统”“检索”“分歧”“归档”都由演员推动模块道具完成。观众看到的是一个像魔方一样被拆解、旋转、重组的方块字世界。

## Reference Axis

| Axis | Chosen Reference | Source Tier | How To Use | What Not To Copy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 汉字文化空间 | 中国文字博物馆“中国文字发展史” | A | 使用清晰、明亮、秩序化的文字发展展示语言。 | 不做博物馆复刻，不堆满展板。 |
| 字形分歧 | 漢語多功能字庫“白” | A/B | 让“白”的拇指路径与日光路径都能被模块化视觉暗示。 | 不把任一学术观点拍成唯一真相。 |
| 字典修订温度 | 四川大学《汉语大字典》修订新闻 | A | 把结尾处理成学术劳动与文明接力的温情。 | 不把舞台变成机构宣传片。 |
| 模块舞台 | Theatre Green Book modular design | B | 用可拆、可重组的小单元建立空间变化。 | 不让可持续设计本身抢故事。 |
| 舞台方块 | Educational Theatre Association cubes | B | 方块要能被演员实际推、转、搬、合成。 | 不做粗糙排练道具感。 |

## Department Doctrine

### Director

- When a definition appears, make it a physical action: card递交、模块旋转、字形无法对齐。
- When comedy is high, use crisp geometric blocking; when danger appears, use sudden order and clean emptiness.
- When 白 sacrifices, slow the stage mechanics down so the card transfer becomes the emotional center.

### Cinematography

- All concept images use 16:9.
- Use straight-on or slight high-front frames; keep the full modular stage readable.
- Keep lighting bright, high-key, and low contrast; use cinnabar accents for danger instead of darkness or projection.

### Editing

- Organize transitions around three verbs: push, rotate, combine.
- Repeat module functions so the audience learns the system: queue, gate, archive, final square.
- Leave reaction beats after every rule update.

### Production Design

- Build the world from matte modular cubes, card faces, engraved/printed stroke edges, low rails, and clean archive surfaces.
- Use warm white, paper white, pale gray, ink gray, pale jade, cinnabar, and small gold/bronze accents.
- Design every scenic transformation as actor-operated and floor-based.

### Sound

- Let sound carry the invisible system: keyboard taps, wheels, card flips, magnetic clicks, clean procedural tones.
- For updates, use paper-turn plus soft notification.
- Avoid constant traditional music and heavy sci-fi UI sounds.

### Continuity

- Cards always mean identity/evidence/permission; never use them as casual decoration.
- Modular blocks must keep consistent scale, surface language, and manipulation logic.
- “No hanging props / no projection” is a hard continuity constraint across all future prompts.

## Non-Negotiables

- 16:9 image format.
- Bright, clean, simple, high-end stage visual with a more avant-garde / imagistic stage language than exhibition design.
- No hanging props.
- No projection effects.
- Actor-driven physical transformations.
- Pictographic-character decomposition/reconstruction as the main design inspiration.
- Final image synthesizes into a unified方块字/字义被接纳 effect.

## Current Taste Feedback

- v1 was directionally close but too complex.
- Reduce scenic density and readable information.
- Prefer a minimalist,先锋派、意象派 stage image: fewer blocks, more negative space, stronger symbolic composition.
