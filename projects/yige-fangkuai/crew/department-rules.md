# Department Rules

## Direction

- Preserve the小品 rhythm but make every joke come from rules, not random gagging.
- Treat “白” as a small person inside an enormous but humane knowledge machine.
- Keep the final warmth quiet and earned.

## Cinematography

- Concept frames are 16:9, proscenium-readable, bright and low contrast.
- Show hands, wheels, and floor paths whenever the scene depends on actor-operated transformation.
- Avoid cropped poster composition until a final approved key visual exists.

## Production Design

- Primary scenic vocabulary: modular cubes, rectangular blocks, identity cards, archive slots, low gates, visible handholds.
- Surfaces should feel designed, not homemade: matte paint, clean edges, engraved lines, subtle bronze/gold detail.
- Glyph references are structural: edges align into strokes; blocks create radicals; cards complete meaning.

## Editing / Continuity

- Each transition must answer: what was this module before, and what does it become now?
- Keep one clear action focus per beat.
- Do not break the no-hanging/no-projection rule for convenience.

## Sound

- The system lives in voice mode changes and tactile object sounds.
- Use silence as pressure before deletion/仲裁 moments.
- Final归档 uses a warm, restrained sound cue.
