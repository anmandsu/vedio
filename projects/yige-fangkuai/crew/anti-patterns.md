# Anti-Patterns

- Projection calligraphy, LED walls, hologram UI, or screen-heavy cyber-system visuals.
- Hanging scrolls, suspended radicals, overhead banners, or flying scenic elements.
- Dark cyberpunk dictionary room.
- Antique brown “traditional culture” clutter.
- Literal museum display copied onto stage without actor interaction.
- Long abstract prompts that say “traditional, elegant, cultural” without physical references.
- Costumes that turn all characters into generic古风 figures.
- Cards used as confetti or decorative texture instead of identity/evidence/permission.
- Final image that only shows a pretty stage but does not express拆解、重构、合成、归档.
