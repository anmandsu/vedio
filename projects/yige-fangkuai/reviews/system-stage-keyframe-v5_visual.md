## Passes

- Combines v3's clean background and negative space with v4's lighter, more refined module design.

- 16:9 aspect ratio is correct.
- No projection, LED screen, hanging prop, or dense exhibition wall.
- Actor-operated transformation is clear.
- Central structure keeps a restrained square-character / 白-like implication without becoming literal or busy.
- Identity card clue is minimal and does not clutter the stage.

## Remaining Note

- The central module still reads slightly as a large architectural doorway/frame. In later shot planning, block choreography should add more square-character assembly actions so it does not become a static portal.

## Recommendation

Use v5 as the current system-stage master visual direction. Future variants should preserve:

- pure paper-white background,
- lightweight translucent-edge modular frames,
- visible casters/supports,
- minimal actor count,
- restrained identity card,
- no projection/no hanging/no dense labels.
