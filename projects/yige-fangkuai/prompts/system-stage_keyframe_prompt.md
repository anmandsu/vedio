# System Stage Keyframe Prompt Draft

## Purpose

First 16:9 concept image for the overall stage design of 《一个方块》. This is a prompt draft only; do not generate until research and visual bible review pass.

## Research Observations To Cite

- Chinese Writing Museum reference: bright, clean cultural display of Chinese writing development, with clear structure and disciplined exhibition surfaces.
- Theatre modular design reference: smaller units recombine into different shapes and functions.
- Stage cube reference: actor-playable cubes with practical handholds can become scenic elements.
- User direction: no hanging props, no projection effects, actor-driven modular transformation, Rubik's-cube-like character decomposition and recombination.

## Prompt

Wide 16:9 proscenium stage design render for a contemporary Chinese stage sketch, bright high-key lighting, advanced simple clean visual style. The stage is an abstract interior of a Chinese dictionary revision system, built entirely from physical modular cubes and rectangular blocks on the floor, no hanging props, no projection, no LED screen. Warm paper-white and pale gray matte surfaces, ink-gray edge lines, pale jade archive accents, small cinnabar warning marks, restrained bronze/gold details. Actor-operated modular blocks with intentional handholds and low clean wheels, arranged like a Rubik's cube being dismantled and recombined. Some block faces become large identity cards, archive slots, sorting gates, and pictographic character fragments inspired by oracle bone and seal-script structure. The composition suggests Chinese pictographic characters being split, rotated, sliced, and recombined into a final square-character form, connected to 汉语大字典 and 方块字 culture. Clean open floor, clear traffic lanes for performers pushing props, full stage width visible, elegant museum-like Chinese writing atmosphere, modern theatrical minimalism, tactile physical set design, no clutter, no dark cyberpunk, no antique-heavy brown decor.

## Negative Prompt

Projection mapping, LED wall, hologram UI, suspended calligraphy, hanging scrolls, flying props, ceiling rigging, dark cyberpunk room, cluttered library, antique market, excessive calligraphy wallpaper, messy paper confetti, ornate palace set, low-quality rehearsal boxes, plastic toy cubes, unreadable chaotic text, cramped crop, portrait aspect ratio.
