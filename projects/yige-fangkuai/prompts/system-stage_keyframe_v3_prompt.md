# System Stage Keyframe V3 Prompt

## Direction

Keep v2's minimalist avant-garde stage. Add only one restrained Chinese-character clue so the image reads as 方块字 / 白 / identity acceptance without becoming an exhibition.

## Prompt

Wide 16:9 avant-garde minimalist stage design concept for a contemporary Chinese stage sketch. Keep the composition extremely clean, bright, high-end, and imagistic: warm paper-white floor and backdrop, abundant negative space, soft high-key lighting, low contrast, refined theatrical silence. Only six to eight large matte modular blocks on hidden low wheels, actor-operated, arranged in the center as an almost-formed abstract square Chinese character. The blocks subtly suggest the structure of the character 白 through pure geometry: an outer square-like frame with one top stroke module and one inner rectangular void, not literal printed text. One small missing block is being rotated into place by a performer, expressing a meaning being accepted into the dictionary. Three or four performers in simple neutral stage costumes push, hold, and align the blocks with quiet precision. One restrained identity card sits on or beside the missing block: warm white paper, a tiny cinnabar seal mark, no readable text. Very subtle ink-gray edge lines and one pale jade side accent; restrained bronze detail only as a tiny seal-like mark. The visual inspiration is Chinese pictographic writing and oracle-bone reconstruction, expressed through physical modular geometry and stage choreography. No museum display wall, no shelves, no dense labels, no readable Chinese text, no projection, no LED screen, no hanging props, no suspended scrolls, no clutter. Sophisticated, simple, avant-garde, imagistic, theatrical, clean, poetic.
