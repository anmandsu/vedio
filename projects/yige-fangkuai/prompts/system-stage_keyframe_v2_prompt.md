# System Stage Keyframe V2 Prompt

## Direction

Simplify from v1. Keep the high-end clean physical stage direction, but remove exhibition density. Make it more avant-garde, imagistic, symbolic, and stageable.

## Prompt

Wide 16:9 avant-garde minimalist stage design concept for a contemporary Chinese stage sketch. A clean bright empty proscenium stage with abundant negative space, high-end simple visual language, warm paper-white floor and backdrop, soft high-key lighting, low contrast, airy and refined. Only a small number of large matte modular cubes and rectangular blocks on hidden low wheels, arranged as an abstract deconstructed Chinese square character in progress. The blocks are physical, actor-operated, with subtle handholds and ink-gray edge lines; they feel like parts of a Rubik's cube being separated and recombined. Three or four performers in simple neutral stage costumes gently push and rotate the blocks, creating the feeling of pictographic character strokes being cut apart and reassembled. The visual inspiration is Chinese pictographic writing and oracle-bone structure, but expressed through pure geometry, silence, spacing, and symbolic stage composition, not museum display. One central almost-formed square glyph silhouette suggests the character 白 / a meaning being accepted, with one missing or rotating block creating poetic tension. Minimal cinnabar accent on one identity card, pale jade archive accent on one block edge, restrained bronze detail only as a tiny seal-like mark. No readable text except abstract glyph marks. No walls full of labels, no shelves, no exhibition panels, no projection, no LED screen, no hanging props, no suspended scrolls, no clutter. Sophisticated, simple, avant-garde, imagistic, theatrical, clean.

busy museum exhibit, dense wall panels, many readable Chinese labels, library shelves, archive cabinet wall, clutter, projection mapping, LED screen, hologram UI, hanging calligraphy, suspended props, flying scrolls, ornate antique decor, dark cyberpunk, heavy black ceiling rig, crowded performers, messy paper, toy-like cubes, realistic museum photo, poster layout, portrait aspect ratio.
