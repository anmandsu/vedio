# System Stage Keyframe V6 Rubik Prompt

## Direction

Client asks for stronger Rubik's cube feeling and clearer拼接感. Keep v5's premium minimalist material language, but replace the large doorway feeling with interlocking cube/frame units that visibly rotate, separate, slide, and recombine.

## Prompt

Wide 16:9 avant-garde minimalist stage design concept for a contemporary Chinese stage sketch. Pure warm paper-white proscenium stage, seamless blank background, abundant negative space, soft high-key lighting, low contrast, premium and clean. Center stage: a large deconstructed Rubik's-cube-like modular glyph sculpture made from many interlocking square units, arranged as an abstract almost-formed Chinese square character structure. The units are not colorful toy cubes; they are refined matte-white and pale-gray architectural modules with translucent pale-jade acrylic edges, slim ribs, precise seams, visible joinery, small caster bases, and elegant internal voids. Clear拼接感: each square unit has visible separation lines, rotation axes, quarter-turn offsets, slide-lock joints, and slight staggered depths, showing that the character can be dismantled and recombined like a Rubik's cube. Three or four performers in simple off-white stage costumes physically push, rotate, and align different units; one performer turns a square module ninety degrees into the missing position. The whole structure subtly suggests Bai / 方块字 being assembled, but remains abstract and poetic. One tiny warm-white identity card with a cinnabar seal sits near the missing module. No readable text. No projection, no LED screen, no hanging props, no suspended objects, no background calligraphy. Minimal, high-end, avant-garde, imagistic, physical, stageable, with strong modular assembly logic.

Hard exclusions: colorful Rubik toy colors, plastic toy blocks, heavy solid crates, cabinet blocks, doorway-only frame, museum wall, shelves, dense labels, readable Chinese text, projection mapping, LED wall, hologram, hanging props, suspended scrolls, floating objects, brush-stroke backdrop, dark cyberpunk, ornate antique decor, clutter.

## Negative Prompt

colorful toy Rubik's cube, primary colors, plastic blocks, children's toy, heavy crates, storage boxes, cabinet wall, static doorway frame, busy museum exhibit, dense wall panels, readable labels, calligraphy backdrop, ink wash wall, projection, LED screen, hologram UI, hanging scrolls, suspended props, flying objects, dark rigging, clutter, ornate antique decor, portrait aspect ratio.
