# Director Research

## Tone Thesis

这不是“传统文化科普布景”，也不是“科幻系统屏幕秀”。它是一个明快、物理、演员驱动的舞台寓言：字义被拆开、移动、争论、重组，最后被接纳。

## Rules

- When information is dense, turn it into action: a definition is a card, a分歧 is two blocks failing to align, a系统更新 is a physical flip/rotation.
- When comedy is active, keep movement crisp and geometric; actors should look like they are operating a living dictionary mechanism.
- When the threat of deletion appears, do not add horror spectacle; make the space suddenly over-clean, ordered, and procedural.
- When 白 chooses sacrifice, slow down the choreography and let the audience see the card transfer clearly.
- When the ending arrives, frame the administrators/humans indirectly through warmth and rule change; do not introduce literal offstage scholars as characters.

## What Not To Do

- Do not turn the stage into a hanging scroll installation.
- Do not rely on projections to show文字演变.
- Do not cover the clean stage with too many calligraphy marks.
- Do not make every costume古风; the world should feel like an abstract contemporary dictionary system.
