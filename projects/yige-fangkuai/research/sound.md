# Sound Research

## Sound Thesis

Sound should replace projection as the invisible system layer. The stage stays physical and clean; the system is heard through typing, card flips, block wheels, soft mechanisms, and precise voice-mode changes.

## Rules

- Use keyboard sounds at login, but keep them light and rhythmic, not sci-fi heavy.
- Give each module movement a clean tactile sound: wheels, soft wooden knocks, card flips, magnetic clicks.
- For deletion / screening, use silence plus one dry procedural tone rather than horror music.
- For system updates, use a short paper-and-data hybrid cue: page turn + soft notification.
- For final归档, bring in a warm low chime or breath-like tone, allowing the voiceover to carry the meaning.

## Avoid

- No projected UI beeps that imply screen visuals.
- No over-traditional guqin wash under every Chinese-culture moment.
- No constant underscoring that fights the小品台词节奏.
