- Tier: A/B
- Author/speaker: 香港中文大学人文电算研究中心 / 漢語多功能字庫
- Date: accessed 2026-05-08
- Department: script research / visual glyph logic / props
- Claim supported: “白” has oracle, bronze, seal, bamboo-slip and later form references; the page records the 郭沫若 view that oracle/bronze forms resemble a thumb and connect to “伯.”
- Evidence paraphrase: The entry lists glyph examples for 小篆、金文、甲骨、简帛文字 and states that oracle/bronze forms resemble a thumb according to 郭沫若, later borrowed for “white,” with extension toward “伯.”

- Confidence: Medium-high.
- Limits: The page also preserves competing interpretations; do not flatten disagreement into one certainty.
