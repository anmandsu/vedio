# Production Design Research

## Design Thesis

用“方块字的可拆装性”替代电子投影。舞台不是屏幕系统，而是一个由实体方块、卡片、抽屉、边框和字形笔画模块组成的字典后台；演员推动模块完成检索、审判、分歧、归档和最终合字。

## Source-Backed Rules

- When the scene needs to show “汉字文明,” use a clean museum-display grammar: paper-white surfaces, clear panels, visible character evolution lines, and disciplined negative space, because 中国文字博物馆 frames writing history through structure and evolution rather than decorative nostalgia.
- When the system reorganizes information, use modular cubes or rectangular block units that can recombine into shelves, gates, queues, and glyph fragments, because modular stage design supports smaller units that combine into different shapes.
- When actors transform space, give modules handholds, low-profile wheels, and two-sided/three-sided surfaces, because practical theatre cubes need to be actor-playable, sturdy, and reconfigurable.
- When designing “白,” preserve two possible reading paths: thumb/body-part geometry and sun/light geometry, because the script's conflict depends on multiple glyph interpretation routes.
- When the stage gets emotional, reduce objects instead of adding spectacle: remove extra modules until only 白, 大天白亮, one card, and a bright归档区 remain.

## Color / Material System

- Base: warm white, rice-paper white, pale gray, ink gray.
- Bright accents: cinnabar red for system warnings, pale jade for archive order, soft gold/bronze for cultural memory.
- Materials: matte painted plywood, translucent acrylic edges, paper cards, shallow engraved strokes, brushed aluminum rails, rubber wheels.
- Avoid: dark cyberpunk LED walls, antique-heavy brown sets, dangling banners, projected calligraphy, cluttered library realism.

## Modular Set Vocabulary

- `unit_cube`: actor-pushed cube, 45-60 cm stage scale, handholds on two sides.
- `card_face`: one face of a cube can become a dictionary identity card.
- `stroke_edge`: black/gray edge lines that align into oracle-bone or seal-script inspired fragments.
- `archive_wall`: cubes stack into a low wall or sorting shelf, never high enough to require hanging/rigging.
- `screenless_update`: system updates are shown by actors flipping card faces, rotating blocks, or revealing embedded labels.
- `final_square`: modules rotate into a clean 16:9 tableau where a square-character composition reads as “白被接纳 / 方块字成立.”
