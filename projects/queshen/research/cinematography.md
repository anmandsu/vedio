# Cinematography Research

## SC01 Visual Problem

The scene has two simultaneous realities: the ordinary underground casino everyone sees, and Lin Feng's vision-enhanced reality where table, sleeve, and threat become readable.

## Lighting Rules

- When the scene begins, use low-key practical light with the table as the motivated center: an overhead hard pool on the mahjong table, dim red/gold practicals in the background, smoke catching highlights.
- When Lin Feng opens the Nine Nether vision eye, add a controlled gold glint from inside the pupil and a brief transparent scan effect across table/sleeve; the room should not become bright fantasy.
- When Xiaoque appears, her glow should be localized and soft, visible to camera and Lin Feng but not acting as environmental light on other people.
- When Knife Brother dominates, let his dark red suit and cigar ember catch warm highlights while his eyes remain partly under shadow.

## Camera Rules

- Start with oppressive geography: table center, Lin Feng trapped opposite Knife Brother, batters behind Knife Brother and along the side exits.
- Use macro inserts for actionable objects: three-wan need, concealed sleeve, thumb ring tapping, hand touching knife, nine-dot tile.
- Use composed push-ins for mental turns, not random handheld. Handheld belongs to Lin Feng's pre-vision panic and the moment he is physically threatened.
- Use Lin Feng's POV sparingly; the transparent table/sleeve reveal should be one clear subjective shot, then cut back to his face so the audience watches him calculate.

## Physical Reference Observations

- Casino table photos show felt/table surfaces create strong color planes; use dark green or black felt, warm wood edge, glossy tile reflections.
- Mahjong tile photos show thick rectangular pieces with reflective edges; the tile face must be readable in close-up.
- Pexels dim table references support hands, chips/cards/table objects emerging from darkness rather than fully lit room coverage.

## Anti-Patterns

- No abstract golden fog replacing the actual gambling table.
- No neon cyberpunk room unless later research justifies it.
- No constant glowing eyes; the gold should spike at key perception moments.
