# Production Design Research

## Scene Doctrine

The VIP underground casino should feel expensive but rotten: luxury material under criminal pressure. It is not a public casino floor; it is a private room where violence is close enough to touch.

## Spatial Rules

- Central mahjong table under a hard overhead pool; players and money stay inside the light, guards remain half outside it.
- Knife Brother's side has status props: leather chair, cigar ashtray, red suit, gold chain, emerald thumb ring.
- Lin Feng's side has fallen-status details: worn black shirt, no entourage, sweat, tense hands, old dignity in ruined clothes.
- Exits should be visible but controlled by Knife Brother's men; the geography must say Lin Feng cannot simply walk away.

## Material And Color

- Table: dark green/black felt or polished dark wood, glossy tile reflections, warm edge highlights.
- Room: black lacquer, red-brown leather, brass/gold, smoke, low amber practicals, dirty carpet or patterned floor.
- Hero VFX accent: pure gold only for the Nine Nether vision eye and Xiaoque's wings, so the supernatural language stays readable.

## Prop Rules

- Three-wan tile: the missing winning tile, hidden in Knife Brother's left sleeve; it must be readable in POV.
- Nine-dot tile: the tile Lin Feng grabs and slams; it becomes the reversal object.
- Emerald thumb ring: sound and status anchor for Knife Brother.
- Cigar: smoke/time pressure, not just villain decoration.
