# Sound Research

## Sound Bed

The room should be quieter than expected: distant gambling ambience muted behind the VIP door, then the close sounds of this table dominate.

## Sound Rules

- Use Knife Brother's emerald thumb ring tapping as the pressure metronome before Lin Feng decides.
- Let mahjong tiles have weight: dry ceramic/plastic scrape, finger tension, and one heavy table slam.
- Under Lin Feng's vision-eye moment, thin the room into a low-frequency hush plus glassy scan shimmer, then restore table sounds when he returns to ordinary reality.
- Xiaoque's voice should be close to Lin Feng's ear but spatially floating, not room-reverbed like everyone else.
- After “我胡了！”, let the tile impact cut through music and room bed; then leave a half-beat of shocked silence for the next scene/shot.

## What To Avoid

- No constant trailer boom under every shot.
- No cute magical chime every time Xiaoque moves; her first beat is tactical, not decorative.
- No casino crowd walla so loud that Lin Feng's inner calculation disappears.
