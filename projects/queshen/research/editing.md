# Editing Research

## Rhythm Shape

SC01 should cut as a pressure trap, then a perception reveal, then a decision snap.

## Cut Rules

- Before the vision reveal, cut on pressure sounds: tile squeeze, cigar breath, ring tap, knife grip.
- During the vision reveal, slow the cutting for one subjective scan so the audience understands where the hidden three-wan is.
- After Xiaoque warns Lin Feng, accelerate through thought and object inserts: her warning, his eyes, sleeve, knife hands, nine-dot draw, slam.
- Each line of voiceover should land over visual evidence, not neutral face coverage.

## Continuity Rules

- Maintain table axis: Lin Feng on one side, Knife Brother opposite, Xiaoque above/near Lin Feng's shoulder and then above table.
- Keep the hidden tile in Knife Brother's left sleeve consistently on screen-left or screen-right based on the established axis.
- Preserve the sound bridge of ring tapping until Lin Feng chooses action; after the tile slam, cut sound to impact and breath.

## Borrowed Lessons

- `Rounders` supports intimate gambling language through voiceover and table sounds.
- `Casino Royale` supports making card play cinematic through reaction chains and suspenseful information release.
