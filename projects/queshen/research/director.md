# Director Research

## Goal For SC01

Make the opening casino scene feel like a death trap that turns into a hero-rebirth scene. The audience should understand the rules of the table fast enough to enjoy Lin Feng's reversal, even if they do not understand mahjong deeply.

## Craft Rules

- When the audience needs to understand the game state, show only the actionable rule: Lin Feng needs three-wan, Knife Brother has hidden it, direct exposure gets him killed.
- When Lin Feng gains information through the Nine Nether vision eye, switch from victim coverage to hunter coverage: steadier frame, colder face, tighter attention to hands and sleeves.
- When Xiaoque appears, stage the room so only Lin Feng's eyeline acknowledges her; every other actor continues the previous pressure pattern.
- When the scene risks becoming exposition-heavy, let voiceover carry inner logic while the image stays on physical evidence: tile, sleeve, sweat, knife hand, tapping thumb ring.
- When borrowing from gambling-film tradition, use table presence and mythic confidence; avoid comedy-parody reaction beats.

## What To Borrow

- From `Casino Royale`: the card-table scene as a duel made from faces, hands, controlled push-ins, and information pressure.
- From `Rounders`: voiceover as a way into gambling psychology and the sound of the room.
- From `赌神`: the hero's sudden aura shift at the table, where gambling becomes performance and identity.

## What Not To Copy

- Do not copy Western poker rules or tournament glamour; this is a Chinese mahjong death game.
- Do not turn Xiaoque into a broad comic relief beat; her first function is tactical survival.
- Do not over-explain the whole revenge premise in this scene. The opening only needs survival, cheating, and reversal.
