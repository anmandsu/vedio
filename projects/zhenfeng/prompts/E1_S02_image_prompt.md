## Category: Portrait (medium shot, interior)

## Prompt

35mm film photography, Chengdu late afternoon, warm golden light through west-facing window at ~3200K, the light is warm but fading — "warmth that is leaving." Old Chengdu embroidery workshop interior.

Shot 1 (empty frame establishing): A red demolition notice posted on the aged wall near the entrance. The paper is cheap printed red, harsh against the warm off-white wall. Four large characters "明日执行" (Execute Tomorrow) catch the golden side-light — stark and threatening. The rest of the wall shows peeling patches and years of small nails. Documentary stillness.

Shot 2 (single character, door entry): A young Chinese woman (26 years old) bursts through the old wooden door on the right side of frame. She carries a baby in one arm. She's wearing jeans and a light-colored casual jacket over a simple shirt. Mid-length hair, slightly messy, tied back. No makeup. Her movements are quick — not angry, urgent. She slaps her phone down on a small side table near the embroidery frame. The phone screen glows with short-video platform data (post-production overlay). Doorway composition: the old doorframe acts as a natural frame-within-frame, the woman entering from right.

Shot 3 (single character, static observation): The older woman (mother) continues embroidering at her frame, head not lifting. Shot from behind the embroidery frame, through the edge of stretched silk — obstructed-view composition. She wears the same dark navy duijin shirt. Her hands maintain the rhythm of needlework — refusing to stop. The warm light catches her left side profile. She doesn't look up.

Space: Gray brick floor. On the back wall, the wooden sign "蜀绣" watches over everything. North wall silk threads catching the last warm light. The space has the clutter of decades — not messy, lived-in. Worn bamboo chair near the entrance. Old tea table.

Color: dominated by wood brown + off-white walls. The red notice is the only aggressive color. Warm golden light from right window, fading. Dark shadows growing from the left.

Authentic 35mm film grain. No plastic skin. Documentary naturalism. Restrained performance — no theatrical expressions.

## Negative Prompt

no Japanese room, tatami, shoji. no qipao. no three-person wide shot. no theatrical shouting, no exaggerated entrance. mother must NOT look up. no music visualization. no warm cozy atmosphere — the warmth is leaving. no watermark, no text.
