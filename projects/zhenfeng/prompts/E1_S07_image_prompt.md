# Image Prompt: E1_S07 — 现实·屏幕亮起

## Reference Cases
- portrait/case1 — mixed light sources, cool screen light on face

## Category: Portrait (medium, interior night, mixed light)

## Prompt

35mm film photography. Back in the main workshop. Same deep night as E1_S05. Transition moment.

**Shot 1** (lin-suqin standing): The older woman stands in the workshop, still carrying the emotional residue of the flashback memory. Her posture is slightly unmoored — not dramatic, just someone who has just surfaced from a deep memory. The warm desk lamp (~2800K) still glows on her right. She stands near the embroidery frame.

**Shot 2** (xiaowei connects laptop): The young man (gray hoodie, glasses) quietly walks to a small table, opens his laptop, connects it to a larger screen. His movements are calm and intentional — not a tech demo, a careful preparation. The laptop keyboard clicks softly. The desk lamp is dimmed — the warm amber fades.

**Shot 3** (screen glow): As the warm lamp dims, a cool blue-white screen light (~6500K) rises from the monitor. The screen itself shows a blank soft blue glow (content to be composited in post-production — do NOT generate text or UI on screen). The blue light spills across the room, hitting lin-suqin's face from the front — half her face now cool blue, half still touched by fading warm amber. This is the first color-temperature conflict in the film: warm tradition vs cool technology.

The young man says quietly: "妈……我骗了你。" (Mom... I lied to you.)

The room waits. Screen glows. Light has shifted.

## Negative Prompt

no text/UI on screen (leave blank blue glow for compositing). no theatrical "reveal" lighting. no dramatic shadow patterns. no three-person shot. no music visualization.

## Visual Anchors Embedded
- lin-suqin: standing, slightly unmoored after flashback
- xiaowei: gray hoodie, glasses, laptop, calm preparation
- lighting transition: warm amber dims → cool blue rises (color temperature conflict begins)
