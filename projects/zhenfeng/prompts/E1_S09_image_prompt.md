# Image Prompt: E1_S09 — 崩溃·觉醒

## Reference Cases
- portrait/case2 — adopted solitary figure, deep emotional weight through body language not face
- portrait/case1 — adopted concrete texture, 35mm vocabulary

## Category: Portrait (medium + closeup, interior night, emotional climax)

## Prompt (6 sub-shots, all restrained, no face-crying closeups)

35mm film photography. Same workshop, deepest night. Desk lamp (~2800K warm amber) ON. Screen cool blue glow (~6500K) still present from the AIGC sequence that just ended. Two light sources in conflict — the warm tradition and cool technology — now sharing the same room. This is the visual manifestation of lin-suqin's internal rupture.

**Sub-shot 1** (watching screen — medium): The older woman faces the screen, her back to us or in profile. The blue screen light falls on her from one side, warm lamp from the other. She watches for a long moment. Then quietly, almost to herself: "好看。" (It's beautiful.) Pause. "但那不是蜀绣。" (But that's not Shu embroidery.) Her voice is soft — not dismissive, almost regretful. She turns to leave. Fixed camera, no push-in.

**Sub-shot 2** (she stops — medium close-up): She walks past the side table. Her feet stop. The fu-dai (lucky bag) sits on the table where xiuer left it in E1_S05. She sees it. Her body freezes — the camera stays on her lower body and the fu-dai. Her hand, at her side, trembles. We do NOT see her face. The fu-dai is in the lamp light — faded dark red, frayed edges.

**Sub-shot 3** (needle box falls — extreme close-up, floor): The old wooden needle box tips from the table edge. We see only the RESULT — needles scattered across the gray brick floor. Multiple fine embroidery needles lying at different angles, catching glints of lamp light and screen blue. Some have rolled to the embroidery frame leg. The composition is a still life of collapse. Needles on worn brick. No motion — the aftermath.

**Sub-shot 4** (hands trying to pick up — extreme close-up, floor level): The older woman's hands enter the frame from above. She crouches at floor level. Her fingers reach for a needle — they tremble. She tries to grasp it. It slips. She tries again. Another needle, same result. Her fingertips brush against the needles but cannot close. The hands are the entire emotional performance. Her face is NOT visible — we see only the back of her head and shoulders from a medium-wide perspective. The camera does NOT push in. It stays at a respectful distance, observing.

**Sub-shot 5** (kneeling, broken — medium shot, eye level ~50cm): She kneels on the brick floor among scattered needles. Finally her voice, choked: "我守了一辈子针……是不是……守错了……" (I've guarded the needle my whole life... was I... guarding the wrong thing...) The words come out broken, not shouted. Her shoulders tremble slightly. Her hair has come slightly loose from the bun.

**Sub-shot 6** (confession — medium close-up, face to shoulders only): The young man (xiaowei) speaks quietly from near the screen: "这幅,是外婆没绣完的。我用AI,把它补完了。" (This piece... grandmother never finished it. I used AI to complete it.) The older woman looks up — at the screen, at the fu-dai, her hand rising one final time to touch her left earlobe. Then, barely audible: "娘……我不会传……" (Mother... I don't know how to pass it on...) Tears in her eyes but not streaming — held, restrained, a lifetime of control cracking but not breaking. 35mm, fixed, no push-in, no orchestral swell, just quiet devastation.

After the last word: 5 seconds of silence. Only ambient sound. Then black.

## Negative Prompt

no theatrical crying, no open-mouth sobbing, no face planted in hands, no dramatic lighting shift. no push-in during emotional climax. no music visualization. no camera looking down on her (no俯拍). no needle box falling in motion (only scattered result). no successful picking-up of needles.

## Visual Anchors Embedded
- lin-suqin: hair loosening, hand trembles, touches earlobe, tears held not streaming
- fu-dai: on table, she stops when seeing it
- needle: scattered on brick floor — collapse externalized
- xiaowei: quiet confession near screen
