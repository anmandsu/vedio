# Image Prompt: E1_S06 — 闪回·外婆

## Reference Cases
- portrait/case3 — adopted warm intimate atmosphere, but transformed to Chinese oil-lamp aesthetic

## Category: Portrait (medium, warm flashback, memory aesthetic)

## Prompt

Flashback sequence. Visual language shifts to "memory" mode — warmer, softer edges, slight sepia undertone but not Instagram-filter obvious. Oil lamp as sole light source (~2200K, warmest color temperature in the entire film).

An old Chinese house interior, different from the main workshop — simpler, older. Oil lamp on a low wooden table, flame steady with minimal flicker. Its light creates a small golden pool, edges fading to complete black. The quality of light is different from the workshop's electric lamps — softer, more organic, the shadows have fuzzy edges.

**Shot 1** (grandmother + young xiuer — medium wide): An elderly Chinese woman (~60 years old in this flashback) sits at an older, darker embroidery frame. Her face shares the same bone structure as lin-suqin — same cheekbones, same nose bridge, same way of pinning her graying hair in a low bun — but her expression is softer, eyes warmer. She wears a cotton-linen duijin shirt in a slightly lighter shade (dusty blue rather than dark navy). Her hands move with the same embroidery gesture as lin-suqin — needle held the same way, same finger position — but steadier, calmer. A young girl (~6-7 years old, this is xiuer as a child, or perhaps lin-suqin as a child) lies on her stomach nearby, chin propped on hands, watching the grandmother's hands with simple childish curiosity. Not "studying" — just watching something interesting.

**Shot 2** (grandmother's hands — close-up): The grandmother's hands working the needle. The hands are older than lin-suqin's — more wrinkles, slightly more calloused — but steadier. Same exact needle-holding posture (index finger and thumb, slight bend). The needle enters the silk cleanly, rhythmically. The oil lamp's warm light catches the silk thread's natural luster.

The grandmother speaks while embroidering — she doesn't stop her hands to talk. Her expression is gentle but not saccharine. "蜀绣,是一代传一代的。" (Shu embroidery passes from one generation to the next.)

Memory aesthetic: slightly diffuse edges, warmer color bias, oil lamp authenticity. Not dream-sequence surreal — just "remembered."

## Negative Prompt

no ghost, no supernatural apparition. no Instagram vintage filter. no dramatic oil lamp flickering (steady flame). no grandmother crying or being overly sweet. no Japanese old house, no tatami. no different face structure from lin-suqin — must share bone structure.

## Visual Anchors Embedded
- grandmother: ~60yo, lighter duijin shirt, same face structure as lin-suqin +20yr younger, same hair-bun method, same needle-holding gesture, softer expression
- young girl: 6-7yo, lying on stomach watching, simple curiosity
