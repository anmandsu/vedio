## Category: Portrait (closeup) + Interior (establishing)

## Prompt

35mm film photography, cold gray Chengdu winter morning light through old wooden lattice window, ~5500K natural diffused light from left side, no fill light, deep natural shadows, subtle film grain, an elderly Chinese woman (56 years old) sits at a large traditional embroidery frame in an old Chengdu workshop. Ultra-realistic documentary style, not glamorized.

Extreme close-up macro shot: a fine embroidery needle pierces cream-colored silk stretched drum-tight on a dark cypress wood frame. The needle tip enters the fabric cleanly. Silk thread pulls taut with subtle tension. The focus is on the needle point and fabric texture — the embroiderer's fingers appear as soft blurred foreground at the frame edge, not the subject.

Medium shot: The woman sits with straight back, wearing a simple dark navy cotton-linen Chinese duijin shirt (中式对襟衫), sleeves slightly worn. Hair in a simple low bun, naturally graying, held with a plain black hairpin. No jewelry. No makeup. Face shows calm concentration — not performing, just being. Her left earlobe has a tiny visible hole (old piercing, never wears earrings).

Another extreme close-up: the needle suddenly pauses mid-air, suspended. Her index finger and thumb — slightly calloused from decades of embroidery — freeze. Then the needle descends but lands slightly misaligned, one stitch breaking the otherwise uniform pattern. Her hand instinctively rises to touch her left earlobe.

Background: old Chengdu embroidery workshop interior. Gray brick floor with worn patches. Whitewashed walls with slight yellowing. Dark wooden beams. A large wooden sign reading "蜀绣" (Shu Embroidery) hangs centered on the back wall. North wall: floor-to-ceiling wooden shelves holding hundreds of silk thread spools organized by color — reds, golds, greens, blues — the only saturated colors in the otherwise muted gray space. Thread shadows cast by morning light through the left window.

Color palette: dominated by wood brown (#6B3A2E), warm off-white walls (#E8E0D5), deep gray brick floor. Silk threads provide the only vivid color accents — crimson (#C41E3A), gold (#D4A574). Cold gray morning atmosphere. No warm tones. No golden hour.

Authentic documentary naturalism, no plastic skin, no digital over-sharpening, no airbrushing. Worn textures on every surface — scratched wooden embroidery frame, patched walls, decades of use visible.
