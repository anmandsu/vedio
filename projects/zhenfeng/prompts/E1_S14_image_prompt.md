# Image Prompt: E1_S14 — 结尾·人来人往

## Reference Cases
- poster/case1 (Boston city poster) — adopted warm evening urban glow, layered depth
- poster/case4 (Chinese minimalist) — adopted 新中式 composition, negative space, quiet dignity

## Category: Poster (wide establishing, exterior night, ending)

## Prompt (3 fixed frames simulating a pull-back, NO real camera movement)

**Frame 1** (inside looking at the sign — medium): The wooden sign "蜀绣" on the back wall of the workshop. Warm interior light (~3000K) from multiple lamps now — not just one desk lamp. The sign's carved gold characters catch the warm light and reflect it softly — natural reflection on old gold paint, NOT neon, NOT LED, NOT self-illuminating. "natural reflection of warm light on gold characters of old wooden sign, no neon or LED effect." The workshop behind it is alive — warm, filled with gentle activity.

**Frame 2** (from outside the door — wide): Standing on the old Chengdu street, looking through the open doorway into the workshop. The doorway creates a "frame within a frame." Inside: warm light, the sign visible, figures moving gently (silhouettes, blurred — not identifiable individuals). Silk threads catching light. Outside: the old street, gray brick, evening atmosphere (~4500K dusk). Warm interior vs cool exterior — the workshop is the warm heart of the street now.

**Frame 3** (from across the street — wider): The entire workshop building from across the old Chengdu street. Warm light spills from its windows onto the pavement. Distant silhouettes of people pass by on the street — out of focus, unidentifiable, just the rhythm of life. "distant silhouettes passing, out of focus, warm-lit shop window at dusk." The workshop is one glowing point in a row of old buildings. Above: dusk sky fading from blue to warm.

Fade to black.

After 2 seconds of black: subtitle appears: "她守了一辈子针,差点断了线。" (She guarded the needle her whole life, and almost let the thread break.)

This is NOT a "The End" or a resolution. It's a "life continues." The light is on. People come and go. Someone is inside, embroidering.

## Negative Prompt

no neon lights, no LED signs, no the wooden sign self-illuminating (it reflects external light). no drone aerial shot. no real camera dolly movement — these are three fixed frames, not a motion shot. no group photo, no "family standing together" finale. no "THE END" text. no identifiable faces in the street crowd (silhouettes/blur only). no dramatic golden hour — this is dusk, quiet, ordinary.

## Visual Anchors Embedded
- wooden-sign: warm light reflection on gold characters
- embroidery-workshop: alive, warm, open to the street
- Chengdu old street: gray brick, dusk, ordinary life
- ending philosophy: 是枝裕和 — life continues, not "problem solved"
