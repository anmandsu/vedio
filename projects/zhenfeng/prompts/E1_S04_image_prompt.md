# Image Prompt: E1_S04 — 夜·独坐

## Reference Cases
- portrait/case2 — adopted cinematic minimal, strong silhouette, deep shadow contrast, solitary figure

## Category: Portrait (wide establishing, low-light interior)

## Prompt

35mm film photography pushed to low-light. The same Chengdu embroidery workshop, now at night. No interior lights on. The ONLY light source is cool blue streetlamp glow (~6000K) filtering through the south-facing window. Deep, profound shadows. Tanizaki Jun'ichiro "In Praise of Shadows" aesthetic.

Wide shot: the workshop is empty and vast around one small figure. The older woman (lin-suqin) sits alone in the darkness on the left side of frame — a silhouette barely visible, her form suggested more than seen. The wooden sign "蜀绣" hangs on the center-right back wall — IT catches the cold blue window light, illuminated while the woman remains in shadow. The sign glows faintly — the moral center of the room, still present, still watching.

Close-up: her hand reaches for the embroidery needle on the table beside her — habit. The hand picks it up, then stops mid-air. The needle hovers. Then the hand gently sets it back down. The needle makes a tiny "ding" against the wooden surface (sound only). The hand remains still.

The north wall silk threads — usually vivid — have gone gray in darkness. Their colors exist only as memory now. The embroidery frame is a dark silhouette. The bamboo chair, the tea table — all shapes in shadow.

After 4 seconds of stillness: fade to black.

This is the quietest image in the entire film. The space swallows the person. Cold blue light on the sign says: "The tradition remains. The person is empty."

No face visible. No tears. No performance. Just darkness and one illuminated sign.

## Negative Prompt

no warm light, no interior lamps turned on. no face close-up, no tears, no crying. no dramatic moonlight — this is streetlamp, cold, mundane. no fill light, no rim light (only light source is streetlamp through window). no music visualization. no composition where the person is centered and lit — she must be in shadow.

## Visual Anchors Embedded
- lin-suqin: silhouette only, in shadow, hand stops mid-air
- wooden-sign: ONLY illuminated element, cold blue streetlamp light on carved characters
- embroidery-workshop: night mode, all color drained
