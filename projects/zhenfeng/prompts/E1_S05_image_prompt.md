# Image Prompt: E1_S05 — 深夜·福袋递出

## Reference Cases
- portrait/case3 — adopted warm intimate lighting, fabric texture, soft atmosphere (but NOT Japanese setting)

## Category: Portrait (closeup + medium, warm interior night)

## Prompt

35mm film photography. Same Chengdu workshop, deepest night. A single desk lamp (~2800K warm amber) on the embroidery frame side table creates a small pool of golden light — about 1.5 meters in diameter. Everything outside this circle falls into complete darkness. This is the most intimate light in the entire film.

**Sub-shot 1** (xiuer on the floor): The young woman sits on the cold gray brick floor — she has lowered herself physically, a gesture of humility after the fight. Her right cheek still shows a faint red mark (the slap from E1_S03). She wears the same jeans and light jacket, now slightly disheveled. The lamp light catches half her face — Rembrandt lighting, one side illuminated, one in shadow. She reaches into her pocket and slowly pulls out a small object.

**Sub-shot 2** (fu-dai — extreme close-up): A worn dark red silk lucky bag (福袋), palm-sized, emerges into the lamp light. The silk is old — edges frayed, fabric softened from years of handling, faded from bright red to a deep wine color. A simple drawstring closure. The young woman's fingers pause at the frayed edge — a moment of hesitation, a gentle stroke of the worn fabric — before she extends it forward. Focus is entirely on the fu-dai, not the face.

**Sub-shot 3** (mother's hands — close-up): The older woman's hands rest on her knees. She sits in a wooden chair, still slightly above her daughter. The fu-dai is offered into the space between them. Her hands make a micro-movement — fingers twitch slightly — but do not rise. They stay on her knees. The fu-dai hangs in the air between them. The lamp light falls on both the fu-dai and the mother's still hands. The mother's face is NOT shown.

Warm amber atmosphere. Intimate. Private. The only sound is breathing and silk rustling. 3 seconds of stillness after the offer.

## Negative Prompt

no Japanese lucky charm / omamori style. no bright red new fabric — the fu-dai is faded, old. no mother immediately accepting. no hugging, no crying, no reconciliation. no face close-up of mother. no warm cozy room — the darkness outside the lamp circle must feel vast and cold.

## Visual Anchors Embedded
- xiuer: red mark on right cheek, sitting on floor (lowered posture)
- fu-dai: dark faded red, frayed edges, palm-sized, wine-colored
- lin-suqin: hands on knees, fingers twitch but don't rise
