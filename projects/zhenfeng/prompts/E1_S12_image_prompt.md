# Image Prompt: E1_S12 — 蒙太奇·有人来

## Reference Cases
- poster/case4 — adopted 新中式 framing, doorway silhouette, negative space
- portrait/case2 — silhouette and strong backlight

## Category: Mixed (UI closeup + silhouette portrait, interior/exterior)

## Prompt

**Shot 1** (phone screen — macro): A thumb presses "发布" (Publish) on a smartphone screen showing a video upload interface. Only the thumb and screen visible. The screen glows soft white-blue. Post-production: overlay the video platform UI after generation — leave screen with simple interface placeholder, no AI-generated text.

**Shot 2-3** (phone screens — macro, post-production composite): Like counts rising, comments appearing. These are pure UI overlays composited in post. Generate only the phone-in-hand context — screen blank with soft glow, thumb scrolling.

**Shot 4** (the girl at the door — the most important image): 
From inside the embroidery workshop, looking toward the open front door (south, frame right). A young Chinese woman (~18 years old) stands in the doorway — backlit by bright exterior daylight so she appears as a silhouette, a human outline filled with light. She wears simple casual clothes — t-shirt, jeans, a small backpack. Her face is NOT clearly visible — she is a "contour," a symbol of all the young people who might come. She stands slightly hesitant — one hand gripping her backpack strap. The doorway frames her like a painting: "frame within a frame" (是枝裕和 composition).

Behind her: the old Chengdu street outside — blurred, ordinary, people passing, daily life. In front of her: the embroidery workshop interior — wooden sign "蜀绣" glowing warm on the back wall, silk threads catching light, the embroidery frame with lin-suqin seated beside it (her back to us, or side profile).

The girl's small voice: "阿姨……我能学吗？" (Auntie... can I learn?)

Lin-suqin looks at her. A pause. Then a single, gentle nod. No smile needed. No tears. Just a nod.

The needle continues. The thread does not break.

## Negative Prompt

no girl's face clearly visible (she is a silhouette/symbol). no dramatic "angel rays" through door. no school uniform, no Japanese sailor outfit. no lin-suqin crying with joy, no hugging. no "THE END" text. no slow-motion dramatic entrance. no group shot.
