# Image Prompt: E1_S03 — 傍晚·冲突爆发

## Reference Cases
- portrait/case2 — adopted cinematic minimal portrait, strong silhouette, shadow contrast
- portrait/case1 — fabric texture, 35mm vocabulary, restrained performance

## Category: Portrait (medium + closeup, interior, dramatic)

## Prompt (5 sub-shots, all single-character, cross-cut)

35mm film photography. Same Chengdu embroidery workshop, same fading warm light (~3200K) as E1_S02. The golden light continues to recede — shadows growing deeper. No fill light.

**Sub-shot 1** (xiaowei entry): A young Chinese man (24 years old) enters through the right doorway. He wears a simple dark gray hoodie, glasses (thin frame), carries a laptop bag. His movement is quiet, deliberate — not timid, just unhurried. He holds the laptop carefully. On the screen: a photo of an elderly woman (post-production overlay — leave screen blank with soft glow for compositing).

**Sub-shot 2** (needle pierces fabric — extreme close-up): The embroidery needle freezes mid-air above the silk. A hand suddenly slams the wooden table edge — the needle jerks and pierces through the stretched fabric. Focus on the needle point penetrating silk, a tiny hole torn. The violence is in the result, not the motion. Macro, fabric texture visible.

**Sub-shot 3** (lin-suqin stands — medium shot): The older woman rises from her chair. Her face moves from warm window light into shadow as she stands (back to the window now). She takes one slow step forward. Then another. Her right hand trembles slightly at her side. Her expression: cold, not angry — a person deeply offended but holding everything in. Shot from behind the embroidery frame (obstructed-view composition). Fixed camera, no push-in.

**Sub-shot 4** (xiuer outburst — single medium shot): The daughter stands alone in frame. She points toward the wooden "蜀绣" sign (off-camera right). This is the only moment in the entire film where a character raises their voice. Her face shows the red mark from the coming slap — wait, this is BEFORE the slap. She holds her position, body leaning slightly forward, finger pointing. Raw but not theatrical.

**Sub-shot 5** (aftermath of slap — sequence): 
- Cut to black (0.5s). 
- Baby's face: crying, simple emotion, soft focus background. 
- Extreme close-up: the mother's right hand, trembling, at her side. 
- Medium shot: the mother's back as she walks away into the deeper darkness of the workshop, her hand rising to touch her left earlobe. She whispers so low it's barely visible on her lips: "滚" (Get out). She does not turn around. Fixed camera, observing from a distance.

Shared space elements (same as E1_S01/S02): gray brick floor, wooden sign "蜀绣", silk thread wall, west window golden light fading, red demolition notice.

Standard 35mm film grain. No push-in on emotional peaks. No theatrical crying. No three-person wide shot — ALL shots are single character cross-cuts.

## Negative Prompt

no three-person wide shot, no group composition. no actual slap contact frame (the slap is sound + cut to black + reaction). no theatrical shouting (except xiuer's single line). no dramatic push-in, no handheld shake. no crying face close-up of lin-suqin. no warm reconciliation lighting. no music visualization.
