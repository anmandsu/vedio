# Image Prompt: E1_S10 — 清晨·准备

## Reference Cases
- portrait/case1 — adopted 35mm natural light interior, fabric and equipment texture

## Category: Portrait (medium, dawn interior, new beginning)

## Prompt

35mm film photography. Same workshop. Dawn — new day. Left window light (~4500K, neutral-cool with slight warmth, "the light of a new beginning"). Brighter than E1_S01's cold gray. Not golden. Not cold. Just... morning.

**Shot 1** (xiaowei at computer): The young man sits at his laptop near the embroidery frame. The screen shows a Shu embroidery pattern being digitally reconstructed — lines and nodes in cool blue (post-production overlay on blank screen). He works quietly. His hoodie is the same gray, glasses the same thin frame. He says without looking up from the screen: "AI只做呈现。灵魂在您手上。" (AI only does presentation. The soul is in your hands.) The morning light catches the side of his face.

**Shot 2** (xiuer setting up equipment): The young woman sets up a tripod and phone mount near the embroidery frame. Her red cheek mark from E1_S03/S05 has faded — barely visible now. She adjusts a small LED light panel. Her movements are purposeful but no longer aggressive — the urgency of the fight has become the calm of preparation. She wears a fresh simple shirt. Notes: "amateur lighting setup in a cluttered old workshop, mismatched equipment, phone tripod next to old embroidery frame" — NOT a professional photo studio. The tripod stands on the worn gray brick floor next to the ancient embroidery frame — old and new coexisting.

The embroidery frame sits between them — empty, waiting for their mother. The wooden sign "蜀绣" watches from the back wall. The fu-dai still rests on the side table from the night before (E1_S05/S09). The needle box has been picked up and placed back — needles restored to order (not shown how, just the result).

Color palette: neutral dawn light, wood brown, warm white walls, silk threads catching the first real light of a new day. Not dramatic. Just... morning. A beginning.

## Negative Prompt

no professional photo studio equipment, no clean modern studio space — this is a cluttered old workshop with amateur gear. no xiuer's red cheek mark prominent (it has faded). no dramatic "hope" lighting. no three-person shot.
