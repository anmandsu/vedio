# Image Prompt: E1_S13 — 夕阳·共识

## Reference Cases
- portrait/case1 — adopted warm natural light, fabric, 35mm vocabulary
- portrait/case2 — adopted solitary+group composition restraint

## Category: Portrait (medium + closeup, warm sunset interior, resolution)

## Prompt

35mm film photography. Same workshop. Late afternoon. West window warm golden light (~3200K) — the same window and same light temperature as E1_S02/S03 (the fight scene), but now the emotion is completely different. Warmth that stays. Golden, not fading.

**Shot 1** (empty frame establishing — warm light): The workshop bathed in golden sunset light. Dust motes floating in the window beam. The silk thread wall catches and scatters the warm light. The wooden sign "蜀绣" glows with reflected warmth. This is the same space that was cold gray at dawn (E1_S01), that witnessed the slap (E1_S03), that held the lonely night (E1_S04), that contained the breakdown (E1_S09). Now it holds warmth. The space itself has completed its arc.

**Shot 2** (hands — extreme close-up): The older woman's hand gently adjusts the collar of the baby's clothing. Her fingers move with tenderness — a gesture she has never shown before. This is the most important hand-shot in the entire film (DH3). For thirty years her hands have guarded the needle. Now they are adjusting a child's collar. "From guarding the stitch to passing it on" — said entirely through one hand gesture. No face needed.

**Shot 3** (lin-suqin with child — medium): She holds the baby in her lap, sitting in the bamboo chair near the entrance. The baby's face is turned inward, nestled against her shoulder — NOT facing camera (avoiding AI infant face generation issues). She speaks quietly, not a declaration, just a statement of fact: "针会老。人会老。但只要有人接——线就不会断。" (Needles grow old. People grow old. But as long as someone receives it — the thread will not break.)

**Shot 4-6** (three single-character close-ups, cross-cut):
- Xiaowei (single, 50mm): "我们不让针变新。" (We won't make the needle new.) — calm, steady.
- Xiuer (single, 50mm): "我们让它被看见。" (We'll let it be seen.) — quiet conviction.
- All three face the same direction (west window, golden light). For the first time in the film, they are aligned — not facing each other in conflict, but facing the same light together.

## Negative Prompt

no three-person wide group shot. no hugging, no crying, no dramatic reconciliation. no baby face visible. no "family photo" composition. no the warm light being overly dramatic — it's just sunset in a workshop.

- xiuer + xiaowei: single shots, facing same direction as mother
