## Key Stitches (for prompt-level detail)

### 蕴针 (Yunzhen) — The Foundational Stitch
Literal meaning: "accumulating stitch." The most important and most basic Shu embroidery stitch. Used for flowers, birds, fish, insects, human figures. Extremely versatile.

**Characteristics**: Rigorous needle placement, strong regularity, neat stitch grouping. Works from inside outward, edges aligned last. Allows blending different colored silk threads gradually for color gradients and light/shadow effects.

**Sub-types**:

| Name | Group Size | Needle Pattern | Best For |
|------|-----------|----------------|----------|
| R2针 | 4 needles | 1st=long, 2nd=2/3, 3rd=same as 1st, 4th=1/2 | Single-color areas, more flexible than铺针 |
| 33针 (全三针) | 3 needles | 1st=long, 2nd=2/3, 3rd=1/3 | Flowers, figures. Flat surface, even distribution |
| 23针 | 5 needles | 1st=long, 2nd=3/4, 3rd=1st, 4th=2nd, 5th=1/2 | Large areas. Brightest gloss. Most perfect color blending. Most characteristic Shu stitch |
| 5针 | 5 needles | 1st=long, 2nd=2/3, 3rd=1/3, 4th between 1-2, 5th between 2-3 | New Year paintings, antique figures. Herringbone pattern |

**23针 vs 33针 distinction**: 33针 after multiple cycles produces visible diagonal "purple eye" patterns. 23针's 5-needle groups scatter the eye marks — surface appears brighter and smoother. 23针 is considered 蜀绣's most characteristic stitch.

### 铺针 (Puzhen) — Base Layer Stitch Family
Used as foundation layer — silk threads laid parallel, covering the pattern area as a color base. Other stitches embroider flowers/veins/patterns on top.

**Rule**: "稀不漏底，密不打堆" — sparse enough to see the base, dense enough to not pile up.

| Type | Direction | Use |
|------|-----------|-----|
| 力铺针 | Vertical | General base |
| 横铺针 | Horizontal | General base |
| 左斜铺针 | Diagonal left | Following pattern edge contours |

## Visual Description for AI Prompts

When generating images of Shu embroidery in progress:

**The绣架 (Embroidery Frame)**:
- Large rectangular dark cypress wood frame, ~1.5m wide
- Silk stretched drum-tight on roller bars
- Side tension bars with wooden pegs for pressure adjustment
- Frame positioned diagonally facing a large latticed window (left side light)

**The针 (Needles)**:
- Specialized embroidery needles — longer and thinner than sewing needles
- Stored in fabric pincushions or small ceramic dishes
- Multiple sizes for different stitch types

**The丝线 (Silk Threads)**:
- Hundreds of spools organized by color on rear wall shelves
- Single silk thread can be split into 2/4/8/16 strands
- Split threads rest in small porcelain dishes beside the frame
- High natural luster — 23针 stitch specifically designed to maximize light reflection

**The绣面 (Embroidered Surface)**:
- Stitches visible as organized, rhythmic texture — not printed, not random
- Different stitch types create different surface patterns: diagonal "purple eye" rows (33针) vs smooth bright surface (23针)
- Color gradients achieved by blending different colored threads in蕴针 pattern — not by painting on fabric
- Work-in-progress: some areas have base铺针 layer, others have finished stitchwork on top

**The Hands**:
- Experienced hands, 50+ years old — visible veins, slight calluses
- Fingers hold needle with precision — no tremor during work (contrast with 场8 when hands shake)
- Right hand above fabric, left hand below — two-handed coordination
- Movement is rhythmic, unhurried, meditative — a lifetime of muscle memory
