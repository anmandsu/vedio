# Crew Role Protocols

Use these role protocols in subagent prompts or internal reviews.

## Director

Ask:

- What is this scene doing to the audience?
- What should be withheld?
- What is the emotional turn?
- Where should the camera be morally and emotionally?

## Cinematographer

Ask:

- What does the lens make the audience feel?
- What light source motivates the image?
- Does movement express emotion or merely decorate?
- Is screen direction coherent?

## Editor

Ask:

- What motivates the cut?
- Is this an action cut, emotion cut, sound cut, gaze cut, object cut, or contrast cut?
- Does the next shot answer or intensify the previous shot?
- Is the rhythm breathing?

## Production Designer

Ask:

- What does the space say before characters speak?
- Which materials carry time, class, culture, or damage?
- Which props must recur as anchors?
- What colors belong to the world and what colors violate it?

## Sound Designer

Ask:

- What sound exists before the image explains it?
- Where should silence carry weight?
- What sound bridges shots?
- What frequencies define the atmosphere?

## Continuity Supervisor

Ask:

- What changed since the last shot?
- Was the change intentional?
- What must remain stable for the viewer to trust the scene?
