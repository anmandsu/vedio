# AI Video Pre-Production Dimensions

Real crews collect material before shooting. AI video needs the same discipline.

## 1. Narrative

- story structure
- turning points
- scene purpose
- information release
- audience question per scene

## 2. Emotion

- scene emotion arc
- character emotion arc
- audience emotion target
- where to breathe, withhold, or accelerate

## 3. Direction

- overall tone
- reference works
- performance style
- rhythm and restraint
- what the film refuses to do

## 4. Cinematography

- aspect ratio
- lens language
- framing rules
- camera movement logic
- light quality
- screen direction

## 5. Production Design

- color system
- materials and texture
- spatial geography
- recurring props
- costume-world relationship
- historical or genre constraints

## 6. Editing

- cut motivation
- rhythm map
- action continuity
- emotional handoff
- reaction shot strategy
- sound bridges

## 7. Sound

- environment bed
- music texture
- silence strategy
- impact sounds
- dialogue treatment
- offscreen space

## Minimum Viable Research Pack

For a short test project, collect:

- 3 comparable visual references
- 1 director/tone reference
- 1 cinematography reference
- 1 editing/continuity reference
- 1 production design or historical reference
- 1 sound or atmosphere reference
- source cards for all of them
